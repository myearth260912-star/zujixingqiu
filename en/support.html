Frequently asked questions

Where are my records stored?

Only on this phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There's no cloud sync yet, so deleting the app deletes your records too. Before you switch phones or delete the app, back up to a file in Settings → Data & backup.

What if I get a new phone?

Back up to a file on your old phone, send the file to your new one, then choose Restore from backup in Settings → Data & backup. Records already on the new phone won't be overwritten.

Can't find a city?

Try another name for it. Penang can also be found as George Town. You can search by province too, like Tibet. If it's really not there, add just the country for now and fill in the city later.

How is distance calculated?

It adds up the straight-line distance between the places on each trip. Think of it as the least you traveled, not your actual flight or driving distance.

Why am I seeing a date conflict?

You can't be in two places on the same day. Leaving one city and arriving in the next on the same day doesn't count as a conflict.

Where can I see my routes?

Switch to Chart in the top-right corner to see every route you’ve added on the globe. In the top-left, you can change the route style or hide them.
