Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Before you start

Footprint Planet is developed and operated by Yiming Li. This policy explains what information we handle, how we use it, where it's stored, and how you can manage it.

The most important thing: Footprint Planet currently has no server-side storage. The trips, photos, name, and profile picture you add are stored only on your own device. We can't see them and have no way to access them.

1. What information we handle

1. Trips you add: the countries and cities you’ve been to, dates, notes you write, and photos you add (picked from your library or taken with your camera). Photos are compressed and stored on your device.

2. Location and time in your photos: When you use Import from Photos (on the web, the photos you pick; in the iPhone app, the photos in your library, after you allow access in the system prompt), we read only the location (latitude and longitude) and time stored in them, on your device, to light up the matching countries and cities and record the dates. Photos from the trips you choose to import are compressed, stored on your device, and added to the matching trips. Neither the photos nor their locations are uploaded. Photos without location info are skipped and not saved.

3. Location: Only after you turn on Location, we read your current location once each time you open Footprint Planet, to mark the city you’re in on the globe and check whether you’ve arrived somewhere that isn’t lit up yet. Your location is only used on your device for that session and is never saved or uploaded. You can turn it off anytime in Settings → Privacy & permissions.

4. Preferences: globe style, distance units, volume, name, profile picture, and similar settings are stored on your device. If you use your own photo as a profile picture, it’s cropped into a small image and stored on your device, never uploaded.

We don’t read your contacts, call history, messages, device identifiers, or other apps installed on your phone, and we don’t use any advertising or analytics tools.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the features you see: lighting up the places you've been on the globe, counting the countries and cities you've visited, and making your poster. We don't build user profiles, personalize recommendations, or show ads.

3. Where it’s stored and for how long

Everything is stored on your own device (in the app’s own storage on iPhone, or in your browser’s local storage on the web) until you delete it. Deleting the app, clearing browser data, or switching devices will remove it, so please back up regularly in Settings → Data & backup.

4. Sharing, transfer, and disclosure

We don't share, transfer, or disclose your information to any third party. We don't hold your information ourselves either, except where the law requires otherwise.

5. Managing your information

View and edit: View and edit your trips on the globe and in Footprints, and change your name and profile picture under You.

Delete: Delete a single trip or all records for a country, or erase everything in Settings → Data & backup → Erase all data.

Back up: Back up all your records to a file in Settings → Data & backup, and use it to restore on a new device.

Withdraw permission: Turn off Location anytime in Settings → Privacy & permissions.

6. Children

Footprint Planet is suitable for people of all ages. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children: all records stay on your own device, nothing is uploaded, and there are no ads or third-party analytics. If a child is using the app, a parent can view, back up, or erase the records in Settings → Data & backup.

7. Changes to this policy

If the way we handle information changes (for example, if we add cloud sync), we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent again the next time you open the app.
